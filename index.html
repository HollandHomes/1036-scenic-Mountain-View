Project Conditions & Disclaimers

Secondary Solution & Warranty Disclaimer

Important Technical Clarification: As discussed with the property owner, installing exterior breakmetal flashing is a non-invasive, cost-effective diversion method, but is not the proper or permanent waterproofing solution for this structure. The permanent fix requires disassembling the existing under-deck ceiling/drainage system and installing comprehensive, code-compliant back flashing against the primary framing.

Because bulk water originates behind and beneath the upper decking assembly, exterior sealants, caulking, and diverters cannot guarantee a 100% waterproof seal under heavy rain or hydrostatic pressure. Therefore, this installation carries absolutely NO WARRANTY against water intrusion, leaks, or moisture bypass.

Clearance & Work Standards

  • Service Access Clearance: Flashing will be bent with a 115° inside angle (open cant) and 1/2" drip hem, designed to provide sufficient clearance for a spline roller to insert into the top screen frame at 90° for future screen servicing.
  • Substrate Preparation: Upper mounting leg is bedded in heavy commercial-grade elastomeric sealant with mechanical fasteners, plus a continuous tooled cap bead along the top wood-to-metal seam.
  • Proposal Expiration: Pricing and supplier material holds are valid for 30 calendar days from the date of issue.
